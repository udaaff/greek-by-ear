Greek by Ear

Learn Greek by listening. Audio lessons from everyday dialogues, with prompts in your own language, Russian first.

YouTube channel

Lessons and long listening videos for people who learn Greek, with or without an exam: a prompt in your language, then the Greek phrase, with a pause to say it aloud. Watch on youtube.com/@greek_by_ear.

Lesson app

The same lessons in an Android app, in development: they play in the background, with the screen off, and keep your place.

Greek by Ear Analytics

A small tool of ours that reads the channel's own YouTube statistics for the channel's owner, to see which videos help learners. It reads nothing about anyone else and shares nothing. Details: Privacy Policy.
