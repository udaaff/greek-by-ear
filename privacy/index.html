Greek by Ear

Privacy Policy

Greek by Ear Analytics. Effective 27 October 2026.

What this is

Greek by Ear Analytics is a small program of ours that reads the statistics of our own YouTube channel, Greek by Ear, for the channel's owner. It runs on the owner's computer. It is not offered to anyone else, has no accounts, no website and no server, and shows no ads. It uses YouTube API Services.

What it accesses

Only after the channel's owner signs in with Google and allows it, with two read-only permissions:

  • youtube.readonly: the name of the channel and the list of its videos (titles, identifiers, publication dates).
  • yt-analytics.readonly: the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports: views, watch time, how long viewers keep watching, where views come from, the main search terms that led to the videos, impressions and click-through rate.

These are figures about our own channel and its videos. The program never changes, uploads or deletes anything on YouTube, and does not read comments or any viewer's personal information.

How it uses the data

The figures are used for one purpose: to see which of the channel's videos help people learn Greek, so that the next videos can be better. They are saved as files on the owner's computer and are read by the owner, also with the help of an AI assistant that the owner runs on that computer to summarise them. They are not used for advertising, not sold, not given to anyone else, and not combined with other data about people.

Where it is kept and for how long

On the owner's computer only. The permission token Google issues is kept in a private settings file on the same computer. Figures are kept as long as the owner allows access. Each weekly run first checks that the access is still granted; if it has been revoked, the program stops, and the figures obtained through it are deleted within 30 days of the revocation. Any request to delete them is carried out within 7 days.

Revoking access

You can revoke the program's access to your Google data at any time on the Google security settings page, myaccount.google.com/permissions (also reachable as security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Choose Greek by Ear Analytics and remove access.

Google's policies

Greek by Ear Analytics' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

By using the program the owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Other products

This policy covers Greek by Ear Analytics only. The Greek by Ear lesson app for Android is not released; its policy will be published at this address before it is.
